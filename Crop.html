HEROGRAM

Cut your card out of a screenshot, sized for each social network.

How it works

  1. In the game: /herogram, pick a format, press Take the picture.
  2. Drop the new file from the Screenshots folder here.
  3. The page finds the card on the black background and exports it at the exact size: Stories 1080×1920, Post 1080×1350, Square 1080×1080, Wide 1920×1080.
  4. Save it, copy it, or share it straight to an app. Nothing leaves your computer.
